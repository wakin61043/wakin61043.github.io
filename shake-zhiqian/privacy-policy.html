Shake支籤 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 26 日

Shake支籤（以下稱「本 App」）是搖籤筒、銅錢六爻、吉凶骰、今日小籤與「幫我決定」的娛樂占卜 App。本 App 不需要註冊帳號，沒有廣告，不連網，也不使用任何分析或追蹤服務。開發者不會收到、也不會保存你的任何資料。

一、在你的裝置上處理的資料

所有抽籤、起卦、擲骰與小籤都在你的裝置上計算。下列資料只存在你裝置的本機儲存空間，不會上傳到開發者或任何第三方：

資料何時保存如何刪除
收藏（你收藏的籤、卦、骰子、決定或小籤結果；含你當時輸入的問題或選項）只有你按「收藏」時「我的收藏」逐筆刪除或全部清除；移除 App 也會一併刪除
介面偏好（字體大小、音樂與音效、音量、震動、搖晃感應與靈敏度）你調整設定時移除 App 或清除 App 資料
生日（選填）你在「我的」頁填寫時「我的」頁按「清除」，或移除 App

生日只用來讓「今日小籤」的結果因人而異，以及提醒當天是否沖到你的生肖，計算全部在裝置上完成。沒有收藏的結果不會被保存。

Android 的備份功能或 iPhone 的 iCloud 備份，可能會把本機資料備份到你自己的 Google 帳戶或 iCloud，這由你的裝置設定決定，開發者無法存取。

二、裝置權限與感測器

  • 動作感測器（加速度計）：用來偵測你搖動手機，只在抽籤、擲錢、擲骰的畫面開啟時讀取，App 切到背景即停止；讀數只用於當下判斷是否搖動，不會保存或傳送。你可以在「我的」頁關閉搖晃感應，改用按鈕。
  • 震動：搖動與出結果時的震動回饋，可在「我的」頁關閉。
  • 摺疊手機的開合狀態：在 iPhone Duo 等摺疊手機上，讀取螢幕是否半折與折痕位置，用來調整版面（例如桌面模式、書本模式）；只在當下使用，不會保存或傳送。
  • 分享與檔案：分享結果圖片時，圖片會先暫存在 App 的快取資料夾，再交由系統分享畫面，由你決定傳給誰或存到哪裡。

本 App 不使用網路，也不讀取你的位置、相機、麥克風、聯絡人或其他個人資料。字型、音樂與所有內容都內建在 App 中。

三、兒童

本 App 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會在知情的情況下蒐集兒童的個人資料。

四、免責說明

本 App 的籤詩、卦象解讀、骰子與小籤僅供娛樂與自我提醒，不構成醫療、法律、財務、投資或任何決策建議。

五、政策變更與聯絡方式

本政策如有變更，會更新本頁並修改生效日期。若有任何問題，請透過 Google Play 或 App Store 上本 App 頁面的開發者聯絡方式與我們聯繫。

Shake Zhiqian Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Shake Zhiqian ("the App") is an entertainment app for fortune sticks, three-coin I Ching casting, a fortune die, a daily mini fortune and a "decide for me" picker. The App requires no account, shows no ads, does not use the internet and uses no analytics or tracking. The developer does not receive or store any of your data.

1. Data processed on your device

Every draw, cast and roll is computed on your device. Favorites (only when you tap "Save", including the question or options you typed), interface preferences, and your optional birthday are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to the developer or any third party. The birthday is used only to personalise the daily fortune and to flag a zodiac clash, entirely on the device. You can delete favorites on the Favorites screen and clear the birthday on the Me screen; uninstalling the App removes all of its data. Android backup or iCloud backup on iPhone may back up local app data to your personal Google account or iCloud according to your device settings; the developer has no access to it.

2. Permissions and sensors

The accelerometer is read only while a shake-enabled screen is open and the App is in the foreground, solely to detect that you shook the phone; readings are not stored or transmitted, and you can turn shake detection off. Vibration is used for haptic feedback and can be turned off. On foldable phones such as iPhone Duo, the App reads whether the screen is partly folded and where the fold is, only to adapt the layout (for example tabletop and book layouts); this is used in the moment and never stored or transmitted. Shared result images are written to the App's cache and handed to the system share sheet. The App does not use the network and does not access your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

3. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

4. Disclaimer

Fortune poems, hexagram readings, dice and daily fortunes are for entertainment and self-reflection only and are not medical, legal, financial, investment or any other advice.

5. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if this policy changes. For questions, please use the developer contact details on the App's Google Play or App Store listing.
